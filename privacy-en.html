1. Developer and scope

This policy explains information handling in Pirate Clicker for Android and on the Vasilisk Games website. The developer uses the name Vasilisk Games. For privacy questions or support, contact gamesvasilisk@gmail.com.

This policy covers the game configuration with Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Last updated: October 30, 2026.

2. Game data on your device

Progress and preferences, including currency, items, achievements, raids, expeditions and language, are stored locally on your device. No separate game account is required. The developer does not use a proprietary server to store your game progress.

A local backup can help recover some damaged saves. It is not cloud storage and does not guarantee recovery after device loss or deletion of app data.

3. Advertising and third-party services

The game integrates Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK and uses UMP for advertising privacy choices. Rewarded ads are opened by player action. Test builds may use demonstration ads.

Google documents collection and sharing of IP addresses, app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. IP addresses may indicate approximate location.

Processing depends on SDK settings, available identifiers, region and user choices. The developer does not receive a copy of all this information from Google. See Google's Privacy Policy and the SDK data disclosure.

4. Consent and advertising choices

Where required, UMP obtains user choices before permitting ad requests. Choosing an in-game bonus does not itself replace the consent form.

When UMP requires a way to change your choices, an “Ad privacy options” entry appears in game settings. Use it to reopen the available form. Android may also let you reset or delete your advertising ID.

Changing your choices does not automatically erase information already processed by third parties. Their own controls and policies apply.

5. Local notifications

Reminders for expeditions, daily finds, offline income and returning to the game are scheduled on your device. The developer does not operate a push-notification server.

You can turn off notifications in the game or Android settings. System permission is requested when required. Refusing notifications does not prevent ordinary gameplay.

6. Support requests

If you email gamesvasilisk@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address and the information you include. This is used to answer questions, resolve issues and handle privacy requests. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary identity documents.

Email is provided through Gmail; Google's Privacy Policy applies to processing by that service.

7. Retention, deletion and your requests

Local data remains until you or the operating system remove it. The in-game progress reset clears game progress but is not the same as deleting every app preference. Android app-storage controls can remove local app data; doing so may permanently remove progress.

Support correspondence is retained while needed to resolve the request, handle follow-up questions and meet applicable mandatory requirements. Email us to request deletion of correspondence or information about its processing.

Google determines retention for its data according to data type and purpose; see Google's retention information. Uninstalling the game does not automatically delete data held by third parties.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection. Describe your request by email. We will explain available steps and may ask for relevant information needed to handle it.

8. Transfers and security

Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. This does not mean every local game file is encrypted or guarantee absolute security.

Third-party services may process information outside your country. Their respective policies explain the applicable safeguards and terms.

9. Questions involving minors

The game does not have account registration asking for a date of birth. If you believe a minor's information has been processed unlawfully, email gamesvasilisk@gmail.com. We will review the request within the information and data controls available to us.

10. Visiting this website

This website provides game information, contact details and this policy. Its code contains no advertising scripts, analytics counters or third-party fonts.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical request information as the hosting provider under its Privacy Statement. Other websites linked here have their own policies.

11. Changes

This policy may change when the game or its data practices change. The current version appears here with an updated date. If renewed consent is required, the relevant choice will be requested through the applicable mechanism.
